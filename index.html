Why Styles Disappear & How to Paste

Choose the correct copy method for your email client

Gmail, Outlook Web, or Apple Mail Compose:

Do NOT copy raw HTML code into Gmail or Outlook. Regular compose boxes expect rich formatted text. Click "Copy Visual (Gmail/Outlook)" and press Ctrl+V or Cmd+V inside your email compose box.

Mailchimp, Klaviyo, HubSpot, or Email Marketing CRMs:

Use "Copy HTML Code". Paste the code into a custom "HTML Code Block" or source code editor in your email provider.
